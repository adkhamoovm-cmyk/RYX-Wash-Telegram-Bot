---
name: Order completion recipients
description: Why completion summaries are delivered to every current operator.
---

Send the completed-order summary and photo to the director and every currently registered operator, rather than trying to identify only the operator who entered the order.

**Why:** Orders do not retain the identity of the staff member who created them. Restricting delivery to a presumed creator would silently omit recipients; the owner requested that operators receive the completion summary as well as the director.

**How to apply:** Keep the staff notification behavior consistent for customer and manual orders. If creator-only delivery becomes necessary, explicitly add durable order provenance rather than inferring it from the customer or assignment.
